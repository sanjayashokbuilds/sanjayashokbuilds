## ⏱️ 24-hour builds

Right now I'm doing a series of 24-hour builds: picking a real problem, building a working solution from scratch in 24 hours, and shipping it. The goal is to get faster, sharper and more efficient every time.

| # | Project | Problem it solves | Link |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Life-hours** | Students can't see what spending really costs them. Life-hours prices every choice in hours of your life (time spent + money ÷ your hourly rate), tracks which ones you regret, and logs Apple Pay payments automatically. | [Live app](https://life-hours.netlify.app) · [Code](https://github.com/sanjayashokbuilds/invisible-costs) |
| **2** | **Chola-dates** | Medieval Chola temple inscriptions date themselves by the sun, moon and stars instead of a calendar year, and many were carved with mistakes. I built an astronomical solver on the Swiss Ephemeris, a machine-readable dataset of 24 inscriptions, and a Bayesian model that infers King Kulottunga III's accession date (**6 July 1178**) while jointly estimating which inscriptions were miscarved. It reproduces all 14 dates a leading 1900s epigraphist judged correct and all 5 of his error diagnoses, and shows 4 of his "errors" follow a recording convention (Bayes factor ≈ 10⁶). 53 automated tests. | [Live app](https://chola-dates.streamlit.app) · [Code](https://github.com/sanjayashokbuilds/chola-dates) |

<sub>**Stack so far:** Python · NumPy · pandas · Bayesian inference · Swiss Ephemeris · Streamlit · Plotly · pytest</sub>
